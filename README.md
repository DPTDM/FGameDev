# MAHIWAGA by December Studios

## Game Overview

Mahiwaga is a 2D, top-down fighting game in which players, known as Mythic Hunters, have to eliminate a variety of dangerous mythological creatures and folk monsters from throughout the world. The game starts in the country of the Philippines. It places you in local/provincial areas where you fight and fulfill missions, level up to become stronger, and collect stuff to construct armor and weapons. As you progress through the story, you will meet comrades who will battle alongside you. After you've made enough progress, you'll be able to unlock the ability to travel to other countries and combat the mythological monsters that lurk beyond.

“In Mahiwaga, players combat deadly folklore creatures across the globe, from the Philippines to Japan, in a fast-paced 2D top-down fighting adventure where leveling up, making powerful gear, and recruiting allies are essential for surviving an ever-expanding world of monsters.”
